Ceasefire talks resume

Delegations from both sides met on Monday in the capital to resume ceasefire talks, officials said, after a pause of nearly two weeks marked by renewed shelling along the front line.

The mediating government said the talks would focus on a prisoner exchange and on opening humanitarian corridors for civilians trapped in the border towns.

Neither side commented on the length of the talks. Observers said a first agreement could come within days if the corridors open as planned.
