# Conspectus

*Conspectus* (Latin): a view of the whole; a survey of everything at once.

A side project, built slowly and in small steps. This file describes the idea; nothing here is a plan yet.

## The idea

A web page that gives a high-level view of what is happening in the world: wars, protests and who is involved in each. Agents could run in the background, following the news and keeping the view current, linking events to each other.

It is not a battle map. There are no front lines or battlegrounds, just the whole picture from above, the way an astronaut sees the Earth.

## Map, and maybe a graph

An interactive world map, and maybe a graph view of who is connected to what, ideally the two working together smoothly.

Involvement changes with events. A country can be pulled into a situation by an event, then step out by saying so, or join by choice.

## Malleable

The idea is that the system does not work from a fixed list of situation types. When something new happens, something nothing has looked like before, the agents can describe it and add it.

## Open

How the agents read the news is to be worked out by experiment; what the view shows and how it looks are still to be decided.
