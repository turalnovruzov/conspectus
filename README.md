# Conspectus

*Conspectus* (Latin): a view of the whole; a survey of everything at once.

A side project, built slowly and in small steps. This file describes the idea; nothing here is a plan yet.

## The idea

A web page that gives a high-level view of what is happening in the world: conflicts, protests, tensions and who is involved in each. Agents running in the background follow the news and keep the view current, linking events to each other.

It is not a battle map. There are no front lines or battlegrounds, just the whole picture from above, the way an astronaut sees the Earth.

## Map and graph

Two views of the same picture, working together:

- a **map**, showing where things are happening;
- a **graph**, showing who is connected to what.

Involvement changes over time. A country can be pulled into a situation by an event, step out by saying so, or join by choice, and the view follows each change and keeps its history.

## Malleable

The system does not work from a fixed list of situation types. When something new happens, something nothing has looked like before, the agents can describe it and add it.

## Open

How the agents read the news, what the view shows and how it looks are still to be decided, mostly by experiment.
